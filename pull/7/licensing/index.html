    Licensing and attribution

    ADI-authored code and assets use Apache-2.0. This includes the custom rectangular finger mesh and materials, its 32 × 32 taxel grid, and the USD integration layer for the Robotiq 2F-85. The asset inventory is listed in NOTICE.

    Isaac Lab adaptations and Tesollo geometry retain their BSD-3-Clause terms in NOTICE. The exact Tesollo license is retained in LICENSE_Tesollo.

    The demos obtain the gear and the Robotiq 2F-85 model separately from NVIDIA’s Isaac Sim asset library. The gripper model is attributed to Robotiq and supplied through NVIDIA. These models and their dependent meshes, materials, and textures are not redistributed here and retain their applicable NVIDIA and third-party terms. The repository’s Apache-2.0 license does not relicense them. See Isaac Sim licensing and the additional software and materials terms.

    Documentation images and videos are rendered simulation outputs depicting these assets, rather than downloadable copies of the underlying models.

    Root license files are canonical and are copied unchanged into the built site. See contribution requirements before submitting changes.
